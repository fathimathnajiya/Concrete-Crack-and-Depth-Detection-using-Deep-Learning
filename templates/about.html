Built on Excellence, Driven by Vision

Concrete structures are highly susceptible to cracks, which compromise durability and safety. Early detection and accurate assessment of crack depth are critical for structural health monitoring and maintenance planning. Traditional visual inspections and manual measurements are time-consuming, subjective, and prone to error. In this project, we propose an automated system that integrates deep learning-based crack segmentation, depth prediction, and long-term crack growth forecasting.

A modified U-Net architecture is employed to simultaneously perform crack segmentation and depth map estimation from 2D crack images. Since the publicly available dataset provides only classification labels, we generate synthetic crack masks and depth maps using a combination of edge detection and intensity-based mapping. The model is trained on these derived annotations to predict both the location and approximate depth of cracks. Furthermore, a simple growth model (linear and exponential) is applied to forecast crack depth progression over 1, 5, and 10 years, providing predictive insights into structural deterioration.
